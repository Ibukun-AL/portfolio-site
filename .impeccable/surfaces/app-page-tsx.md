# Surface brief: hiring portfolio (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Experience (a portfolio; the work leads). Audience: recruiters and hiring managers, remote worldwide and Nigeria. Job: believe "ships end to end", then email. Action: Email me. Proof: three personal-project case studies, four roles with the CV's own figures. Constraints: no invented claims; projects labelled "Personal project"; no phone number.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a shipping document: Ibukun is the shipper and every project is a numbered container with its paperwork. It refuses the dev-portfolio default (name, tagline, card grid).

OWN-WORLD: Dock-concrete ground (cool grey #CFD2CF), ink #14181B, carbon-copy form paper #EDEDE6. Containers in muted corrugated colours (deep teal, oxide brown, slate, khaki) with vertical rib texture and stencilled ID numerals. Hi-vis safety yellow #FFD200 is the one highlighter, used only on clickable actions. Big Shoulders Display for headlines, stencil face for container IDs, Barlow for body, a mono only for container IDs, dates and stack lines. Square corners, 2px ink rules, hazard-stripe edge.

STORY: The visitor sees a shipper whose cargo (web, mobile, AI on Spring Boot) arrives intact; believes the work is shipped, not just built; emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with name left and "Manifest, Abuja to worldwide" right. A stencilled headline "Shipped end to end." at display scale across the left two-thirds, lede below, and the yellow Email me action under it. At right a lifted manifest form (shipper, cargo, backend, status: open to remote). Along the bottom edge a row of four container blocks of different lengths (three projects plus past voyages) that break the section boundary into the next section.

FORM: The Bill of Lading, candidate 1 on the ordered list (impeccable's pick), seed:35d47df9cad99cc4. ADAPTATION: the sketch's four coloured blocks used orange and mustard; they become muted oxide and khaki so yellow stays reserved for clickable actions per the Highlighter rule.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
